Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Phrazify ("we", "us") and our app, the Phrazify Bot, handle information. Phrazify is a free English-learning brand. The Phrazify Bot publishes our own English lessons and quizzes to our own accounts: the Phrazify Facebook Page, the @gophrazify Threads account, the Phrazify Pinterest account and the Phrazify Telegram channel.

Information we access

  • Public comments on our own posts (Facebook and Threads): the comment text, the comment ID, and the commenter's public name or username, so the bot can check quiz answers and reply.
  • Our own Pinterest account: our own boards and Pins, so the bot can publish our lesson images as Pins on our boards, and the analytics of our own Pins (views, saves and clicks). We do not access any other Pinterest user's Pins, boards or account data.
  • Our own post statistics (likes, comments, shares and views), for a private weekly report to the Phrazify owner.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, locations, contacts or payment details, and we do not read private messages.

How we use it

  • Comment text is checked by an AI model (Google Gemini) only to decide whether a quiz answer is correct and to write a short reply or hint.
  • The bot likes and replies to comments on our own posts.
  • For our weekly "Top Learners" post we keep a count of correct answers for the current week, with the commenter's first name and last initial (or Threads username). Counts older than four weeks are deleted.
  • We keep the IDs of comments we have already handled, so we don't reply twice. We do not store comment text.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone. Comment text is sent to Google Gemini only for the check described above. Information is stored privately in our own GitHub repository.

Your choices and deleting your data

If you delete your comment, the bot can no longer see it. To ask us to remove your name from a Top Learners post or delete anything else, message the Phrazify Facebook Page or our Telegram channel, and we will do it within 30 days.

Children

Our content is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the public comment details described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Message the Phrazify Facebook Page or our Telegram channel t.me/phrazify.
