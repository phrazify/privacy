Phrazify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Phrazify is an English-learning brand. Our app, the Phrazify bot, posts English lessons and quizzes to the Phrazify Facebook Page, Threads account and Telegram channel, and replies to comments that answer those quizzes.

What we collect

The bot only reads public comments left on Phrazify's own posts, so it can tell whether an answer is right. It reads the comment text and the comment ID. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, or any other personal details, and we do not read private messages.

How we use it

Comment text is checked by an AI model (Google Gemini) only to decide whether the answer is correct, and then the bot posts a short reply such as "✅ Correct!". We keep a list of comment IDs we have already replied to, so we don't reply twice. We do not store comment text.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. Comment text is sent to Google Gemini only for the check described above.

Deleting your data

If you delete your comment on Facebook or Threads, the bot can no longer see it. To ask us to remove anything else, message the Phrazify Facebook Page and we will delete it within 30 days.

Contact

Questions? Message the Phrazify Facebook Page or Telegram channel t.me/phrazify.
